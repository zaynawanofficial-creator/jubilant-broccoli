SEO content writing services for UK brands

SEO content writing is creating web pages, blog posts and product copy that answer what people search for, in a way Google can understand and customers want to read. Good SEO content earns rankings, builds trust and moves readers to act.

At RankRight Media, our writers research the keywords and questions behind every brief, study what already ranks, and then write something clearer, more useful and more on-brand. Every piece is edited, checked for originality and formatted with the headings, internal links and meta data it needs to perform.

What content can you write?

Blog posts and long-form guides, service and landing page copy, product and category descriptions, email newsletters and social media captions — in your brand’s tone of voice, from a friendly local café to a technical B2B supplier.

FAQ

Content Writing questions, answered

What is SEO content writing?

SEO content writing is writing web pages, articles and product copy that answer what people search for and are structured so search engines can understand and rank them — while still being genuinely useful and persuasive for readers.

Do you use AI to write content?

Our content is written and edited by people. We may use tools for research and checks, but every piece is human-written, fact-checked, plagiarism-checked and edited to match your brand voice.

How much does SEO content writing cost?

We price per piece based on length and research depth, or as a monthly content plan. Content is also included in our SEO packages — for example, Premium SEO includes 4 articles a month. Contact us for a quote for your project.

Can you match my brand’s tone of voice?

Yes. We start from your existing content, brand guidelines and customer language, and refine the tone with your feedback on the first pieces.

Will you upload the content to my website?

We can. Content is delivered publish-ready with headings, meta titles, meta descriptions and internal link suggestions, and we can upload it to WordPress or Shopify for you on request.
