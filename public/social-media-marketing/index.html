Social media marketing services for growing brands

Social media marketing is using platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok to build brand awareness, engage your audience and drive traffic, leads and sales. It combines consistent organic content with targeted paid promotion.

At RankRight Media, we plan a content calendar around your goals, create on-brand graphics, short videos and captions, publish consistently and engage with your community. When you’re ready to accelerate, we add paid social campaigns that reach the right people.

Which platforms should my business be on?

The ones your customers actually use. B2B brands usually see the best return on LinkedIn; consumer brands on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. We’ll recommend a focused mix rather than spreading you thinly across everything.

FAQ

Social Media questions, answered

What do social media marketing services include?

Typically strategy, content planning, graphic and video creation, captions, scheduling, community management and monthly reporting, with optional paid social advertising to accelerate growth.

How often should my business post on social media?

Consistency matters more than volume. For most small businesses, 3–5 quality posts a week on one or two core platforms outperforms daily low-effort posting everywhere.

Do you buy followers or use bots?

No. We grow audiences with real content and targeted advertising only. Bought followers damage engagement rates and can get accounts restricted.

How long before social media marketing shows results?

Engagement often improves within the first month of consistent posting. Sustainable follower growth and traffic usually builds over 3–6 months; paid campaigns can speed this up.

Can you run ads on Facebook and Instagram for us?

Yes. We plan, create and manage Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns, set up the Meta Pixel and conversion tracking, and report on cost per result.
