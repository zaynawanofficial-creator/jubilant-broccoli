Website development services built for growth

Website development is the design, build and launch of a website that is fast, secure, easy to manage and built to convert visitors into enquiries or sales. At RankRight Media, every site we build is mobile-first, accessible and optimised for search from the very first line of code.

Your website is often the first impression a customer has of your business. We combine clean design with conversion-focused page structure — clear calls to action, trust signals and fast load times — so your site works as hard as you do.

What kind of websites do you build?

We build business websites, landing pages, e-commerce stores and redesigns on WordPress, Shopify or lightweight custom code, depending on what suits your goals, budget and team. Every build includes on-page SEO foundations, analytics setup and a handover so you can update content yourself.

FAQ

Website Development questions, answered

How much does a website cost in the UK?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether you need e-commerce. We give you a fixed, itemised quote after a free discovery call, so there are no surprises. Tell us what you need via our contact form and we’ll come back with a price.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes 2–6 weeks from discovery to launch. Larger e-commerce sites with many products or custom features can take longer; we agree a timeline before work starts.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Yes. Every site we build is designed mobile-first and includes on-page SEO foundations: optimised titles and descriptions, heading structure, fast loading, image optimisation, schema markup and Google Search Console setup.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We build on easy-to-manage platforms such as WordPress and Shopify and show you how to edit pages, add blog posts and update products.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We can redesign your current site and migrate content carefully, keeping your URLs or setting up redirects so you don’t lose the rankings you already have.
