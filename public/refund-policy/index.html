Refund Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

We want you to be confident buying from us. This policy explains when refunds and cancellations apply.

Before work starts

If you cancel an order before we have started work, we will refund your payment in full.

Consumers’ 14-day cancellation right

If you buy as a consumer (not for business purposes), you have 14 days to cancel under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. If you ask us to start work within that period, we may deduct a fair amount for work already carried out.

Work in progress and completed work

Because our services involve time, outreach and third-party placements, payments for work already completed or delivered (for example, published backlinks or written content) are not refundable. Where work has been partly completed, we will refund the unused portion.

Monthly plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled before your next billing date. Work already paid for in the current month will be completed and reported.

If something isn’t right

If a deliverable doesn’t match what your package describes, tell us within 14 days of delivery and we will fix it, replace it or refund that item.

How to request a refund

Contact us via our contact page with your order details. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 14 days.
