PPC management services that focus on return, not clicks

PPC (pay-per-click) advertising means paying to appear at the top of Google, social feeds and other platforms, and only paying when someone clicks. Done well, it’s the fastest way to generate leads and sales; done badly, it burns budget.

At RankRight Media, we set up conversion tracking first, then build tightly themed campaigns around high-intent keywords and audiences. We write the ads, build or improve the landing pages and manage bids and budgets every week — so you can see exactly what each pound returns.

Which ad platforms do you manage?

We manage Google Ads (Search, Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube), Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok Ads and Microsoft Ads, choosing the mix that matches where your customers are and how they buy.

FAQ

PPC questions, answered

What is PPC management?

PPC management is the ongoing setup, monitoring and optimisation of pay-per-click ad campaigns — keyword and audience targeting, ad copy, bids, budgets, tracking and landing pages — to get the most leads or sales for your ad spend.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your industry’s cost per click and how many leads you need. We’ll estimate a realistic starting budget from keyword data during your free consultation, start controlled, and scale once campaigns are profitable.

Is the management fee separate from ad spend?

Yes. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platform from your own account. Our monthly management fee covers strategy, setup, optimisation and reporting.

How quickly will I see results from PPC?

Ads can start generating clicks and enquiries within days of launch. Campaigns usually become significantly more efficient over the first 4–8 weeks as we gather data and optimise.

Should I do PPC or SEO?

PPC brings immediate traffic while you pay for it; SEO builds traffic that compounds over time. Many clients run both: PPC for instant leads and keyword data, SEO to lower long-term acquisition costs.
