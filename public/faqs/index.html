PPC (Pay-Per-Click)

What is PPC management?

PPC management is the ongoing setup, monitoring and optimisation of pay-per-click ad campaigns — keyword and audience targeting, ad copy, bids, budgets, tracking and landing pages — to get the most leads or sales for your ad spend.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your industry’s cost per click and how many leads you need. We’ll estimate a realistic starting budget from keyword data during your free consultation, start controlled, and scale once campaigns are profitable.

Is the management fee separate from ad spend?

Yes. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platform from your own account. Our monthly management fee covers strategy, setup, optimisation and reporting.

How quickly will I see results from PPC?

Ads can start generating clicks and enquiries within days of launch. Campaigns usually become significantly more efficient over the first 4–8 weeks as we gather data and optimise.

Should I do PPC or SEO?

PPC brings immediate traffic while you pay for it; SEO builds traffic that compounds over time. Many clients run both: PPC for instant leads and keyword data, SEO to lower long-term acquisition costs.

Link Building

How much does link building cost?

Our link building packages start at €3.49 for 2 dofollow backlinks on a DA 10+ site and go up to €64.49 for 10 editorial backlinks from DA 50+ websites with 1,000-word content. Every package is a one-off price with a placement report.

Are your backlinks dofollow?

Yes. Our packages deliver dofollow backlinks placed contextually within relevant content. Profile backlinks in the Standard Boost plan diversify your link profile alongside guest posts.

Is link building safe for my website?

Yes, when it is done the way we do it: manual outreach to real, niche-relevant websites with genuine content. We never use private blog networks or automated link spam, which are what trigger Google penalties.

How long until backlinks improve my rankings?

Links are usually indexed within a few weeks of going live. Ranking improvements typically follow over 4–12 weeks, depending on your competition and how relevant the linking sites are to your niche.

What is the difference between DA and DR?

Domain Authority (DA, by Moz) and Domain Rating (DR, by Ahrefs) are third-party scores from 0–100 estimating a site’s backlink strength. Google doesn’t use either directly, so we also check real traffic, relevance and link profile quality before placing your links.

Will I get a report of my links?

Yes. Every order includes a placement report with the live URL of each link; higher plans add metrics such as authority and traffic for each linking site.

Social Media Marketing

What do social media marketing services include?

Typically strategy, content planning, graphic and video creation, captions, scheduling, community management and monthly reporting, with optional paid social advertising to accelerate growth.

How often should my business post on social media?

Consistency matters more than volume. For most small businesses, 3–5 quality posts a week on one or two core platforms outperforms daily low-effort posting everywhere.

Do you buy followers or use bots?

No. We grow audiences with real content and targeted advertising only. Bought followers damage engagement rates and can get accounts restricted.

How long before social media marketing shows results?

Engagement often improves within the first month of consistent posting. Sustainable follower growth and traffic usually builds over 3–6 months; paid campaigns can speed this up.

Can you run ads on Facebook and Instagram for us?

Yes. We plan, create and manage Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns, set up the Meta Pixel and conversion tracking, and report on cost per result.
