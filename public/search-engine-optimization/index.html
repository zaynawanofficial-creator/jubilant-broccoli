Search Engine Optimization (SEO) services for UK businesses

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the process of improving your website so it appears higher in Google for the searches your customers make — bringing you free, compounding traffic instead of paying for every click.

Your website deserves more than just being online — it deserves to be found by the right people at the right time. At RankRight Media, we specialise in driving organic traffic that actually converts into real business. Whether you’re a small local shop or a national brand, our expert SEO strategies help your website climb search engine rankings and stay ahead of the competition.

With years of experience and proven results, we’ve successfully ranked websites across many different industries. From deep keyword research to building high-authority backlinks — we handle every part of SEO for you. And we don’t just aim for traffic — we aim for revenue-generating visibility.

Let us help you dominate Google, attract quality visitors, and grow your brand online — step by step, strategy by strategy.

What does an SEO service include?

Every RankRight SEO package combines the four pillars Google rewards: technical health (speed, crawlability, indexing), relevance (keyword-mapped pages and content), authority (high-quality backlinks) and local trust (Google Business Profile and citations). FAQ

SEO questions, answered

How much do SEO services cost in the UK?

Our monthly SEO packages start at €299 per month (Basic SEO, up to 5 keywords) and go up to €1,899 per month for E-commerce SEO covering 70+ product pages. Standard SEO at €499/month adds a technical audit, 20 backlinks and a content strategy. Larger or international projects are quoted on our Custom SEO plan.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most websites see first ranking movements within 4–8 weeks of on-page and technical fixes going live. Meaningful traffic and lead growth typically builds over 3–6 months, depending on your competition, your site’s history and how many keywords you target.

What is included in an SEO package?

Every plan includes keyword research, on-page optimisation of your key pages, meta title and description optimisation and a monthly performance report. Higher plans add technical SEO audits and fixes, link building, local SEO, content strategy and written articles.

Do you offer local SEO for UK businesses?

Yes. Local SEO is included from our Premium plan and available on request. We optimise your Google Business Profile, build consistent local citations and create location-relevant content so you show up in the map pack and “near me” searches.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No honest agency can guarantee rankings — Google controls the algorithm. What we guarantee is the work: every deliverable in your package, done to best practice, with transparent monthly reporting so you can see exactly what was done and what moved.

Is your SEO white-hat and Google-safe?

Yes. We follow Google’s Search Essentials and spam policies: no private blog networks, no link schemes, no keyword stuffing. That protects your site from penalties and builds rankings that last.
