Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how RankRight Media (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal data when you visit this website or contact us. We are the data controller for this information under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, phone number, website and message when you use our contact form, call or email us.
  • Client information: business details and account access you provide so we can deliver our services.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) kept by our hosting provider for security.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If we introduce analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide quotes (legitimate interests / steps before entering a contract).
  • To deliver the services you buy and manage your account (contract).
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations (legal obligation).

We never sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Sharing

We share data only with trusted providers who help us run our business — such as our website host, email provider and payment processor — under appropriate data processing terms.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as required for legal and accounting purposes (normally six years).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data, object to processing, or request a copy in a portable format. Contact us using the details on our contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
