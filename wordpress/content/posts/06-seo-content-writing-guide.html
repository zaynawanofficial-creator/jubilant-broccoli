SEO content writing is creating content that answers what people search for, in a way search engines can understand and readers trust enough to act on. In 2026 it has a second job: being clear and well-structured enough that AI assistants like Google’s AI Overviews and ChatGPT quote it. The good news is that the same principles win on both fronts.

1. Start with search intent

Before writing a word, search your target keyword and study the results. Are they guides, lists, product pages or local results? That’s Google telling you what searchers want. Match the format, then beat it on depth, clarity and usefulness.

  • Informational: “what is link building” → a clear explainer.
  • Commercial: “best SEO packages” → comparisons and pricing.
  • Transactional: “buy SEO audit” → a service or product page.
  • Local: “web designer near me” → location pages and a strong Google Business Profile.

2. Answer first, then expand

Put the direct answer in the first two sentences — a definition, a number or a recommendation. Readers stay, Google can lift it for a featured snippet, and AI tools can quote it accurately. Then use the rest of the page to explain, prove and add nuance.

3. Structure for skimmers (and machines)

  • Descriptive H2s that read like the questions people ask.
  • Short paragraphs, bullet lists and numbered steps.
  • Tables for comparisons and prices.
  • An FAQ section for related questions.
  • A “key takeaways” box near the top of long guides.

4. Show E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authority, trust

Google’s guidance on helpful, people-first content rewards content written by people who know the subject. Add first-hand examples, original screenshots or data, a named author with a real bio, and accurate sources. Keep facts up to date and show when the page was last reviewed.

5. Optimise without stuffing

  • Use the primary keyword naturally in the title, H1, first paragraph and URL.
  • Cover the related subtopics and entities competitors mention — that’s semantic relevance.
  • Write a compelling meta title (≈60 characters) and description (≈155 characters).
  • Add descriptive image alt text.

6. Link internally with intent

Every new article should link to the service or product it supports, and older related posts should link back to it. Internal links spread authority and guide readers toward conversion — for example, this guide links to our SEO content writing service.

7. Update content on a schedule

Rankings decay as information ages and competitors improve. Review your top pages every 6–12 months: refresh facts, add new questions and tighten the introduction.

A simple SEO content checklist

  1. Intent and format match the current top results.
  2. The main question is answered in the first two sentences.
  3. Headings cover the full topic and related questions.
  4. Real experience, examples or data are included.
  5. The author is named, with relevant expertise.
  6. Meta title, description and alt text are written.
  7. It links to and from relevant pages.

Conclusion

Great SEO content is simply great content that’s easy to find and easy to understand. Write for the person searching, structure it so they — and the machines — can find answers fast, and keep it current.
