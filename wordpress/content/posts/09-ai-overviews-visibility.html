Customers increasingly ask AI — Google’s AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot — instead of scrolling through ten blue links. These systems summarise answers and cite a handful of sources. The businesses that get cited are the ones whose content is easiest to understand, verify and trust. Here’s how to become one of them.

How AI search chooses its sources

AI answers are built on top of search indexes and trusted data. In practice, the sources that get cited tend to:

  • Rank well already for the underlying query (SEO still matters).
  • State the answer clearly, near the top of the page.
  • Back claims with specifics: numbers, steps, comparisons, examples.
  • Come from a recognisable, consistent entity with real-world signals (reviews, mentions, profiles).

Step 1: write answer-first content

Open each page with a one- or two-sentence direct answer to the main question, then expand. Use question-led headings (“How much does SEO cost?”), short paragraphs, lists and tables. This is the same structure that wins featured snippets — and it makes your content easy to quote accurately.

Step 2: cover topics completely

AI systems favour sources that cover a topic thoroughly. Build clusters: a main service page supported by guides that answer related questions, all linked together. Our own SEO service page links to guides like how much SEO costs and our technical SEO audit checklist for exactly this reason.

Step 3: make your facts machine-readable

  • Structured data: Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, Product/Offer, FAQPage and Article schema.
  • Consistent business details: the same name, address, phone and description everywhere.
  • Clear pricing pages: real numbers in HTML text and tables, not only in images.
  • An llms.txt file: a short summary of who you are, what you offer and your key pages.

Step 4: strengthen your brand as an entity

AI recommendations lean heavily on what the wider web says about you. Keep your Google Business Profile complete and active, earn reviews on trusted platforms, get mentioned in relevant publications and directories, and make sure your social profiles link back to your site. Quality link building and digital PR help here too.

Step 5: show real expertise

Named authors with genuine experience, original insights and up-to-date information signal trust. Google’s guidance on helpful, reliable, people-first content applies just as much to AI search.

Step 6: keep the technical basics right

AI crawlers can only use what they can access. Don’t block reputable search and AI crawlers in robots.txt unless you have a reason to, keep pages fast and server-rendered where possible, and avoid hiding key information behind scripts or images.

How to track AI visibility

Regularly ask AI tools the questions your customers ask and note whether you’re cited. Watch Search Console for impressions on question-style queries, and track brand searches and referral traffic from AI tools in your analytics.

Conclusion

AI search doesn’t replace SEO — it raises the bar. Clear answers, complete topic coverage, machine-readable facts and a trusted brand are what get you cited. Start with your most important service pages and work outward.
