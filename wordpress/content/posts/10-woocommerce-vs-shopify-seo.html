Both WooCommerce and Shopify can rank and sell well. WooCommerce, which runs on WordPress, gives you more control over SEO and content but needs more hands-on maintenance. Shopify is simpler, hosted and fast by default, with some fixed limitations. The right choice depends on your products, your content plans and who will run the store.

SEO strengths of WooCommerce

  • Full control over URLs, categories and permalinks.
  • WordPress is the best-in-class platform for blogs and content marketing.
  • Powerful SEO plugins for schema, sitemaps and redirects.
  • Complete freedom to optimise templates and page speed.

SEO strengths of Shopify

  • Reliable, fast hosting with a global CDN included.
  • Clean themes with solid technical SEO basics out of the box.
  • Less maintenance: no plugin updates or server management.
  • Product schema and sitemaps generated automatically.

What actually makes an online store rank

Whichever platform you choose, the same things move rankings:

  1. Unique product and category descriptions — not manufacturer copy. Our Ecommerce content pack covers 10 products plus homepage and category copy.
  2. Well-structured categories that match how customers search.
  3. Fast, mobile-friendly pages with optimised images.
  4. Product schema with price, availability and reviews.
  5. Helpful content — buying guides and comparisons that earn links.
  6. Clean technical setup: no duplicate URLs from filters and variants.

How to choose

  • Choose WooCommerce if content marketing is central to your growth, you need custom functionality, or you sell services and bookings alongside products.
  • Choose Shopify if you want to launch fast, sell physical products, and prefer a hosted platform with minimal maintenance.
