# 06 — Technical SEO, helpful content and trust
Audit keyword intent and service differentiation; create original English UK-appropriate copy with source-backed claims. Do not fabricate client stats, case studies, office addresses or ranking promises. Implement semantic document headings, crawlable link architecture, unique metadata/canonicals, sitemap, robots settings, noindex for staging/cart/checkout/account/private pages, breadcrumbs, descriptive alt text, OG cards, image sitemaps if relevant, accurate eligible structured data, 301 mapping if migrating URLs, internal linking and 404. Build Insights article and case-study CMS templates (case studies stay unpublished without verifiable evidence). Add About, How It Works, Contact, FAQ, proposal form, Terms, Refunds, Privacy, Cookies and Accessibility; mark legal drafts as requiring review. Include AI search readability but avoid scaled thin content. Record crawl check, page-by-page SEO QA and missing approvals.
